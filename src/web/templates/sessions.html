Welcome to the Arabic Cultural AI Study 👋

Thank you for participating! This study is conducted by researchers exploring how AI large language models (LLMs) handle Arabic cultural contexts. Well-known examples of LLMs include ChatGPT, Claude, and JAIS 2.

What is this study about?

We are collecting natural, multi-turn conversations between people and AI assistants on Arabic-centric culturally relevant topics. Your conversations will help us understand how well these models reflect authentic Arab culture, values, and everyday life.

What you'll do

  1. Complete a short demographic survey (one time only).
  2. Choose a session from your assigned list. Each has a set topic, country, and number of turns.
  3. Chat naturally with four AI assistants responding to the same question.
  4. Pick your favourite response and rate it on cultural correctness and fluency.
  5. Repeat until the session ends.
  6. Do 2–5 until your session list is complete.

A few tips

  • Write as you naturally would.
  • Be specific and draw from real cultural experiences when you can.

Your responses directly contribute to building AI systems that better understand and represent Arabic culture. Every conversation counts. Your data is kept confidential and used for research purposes only.
